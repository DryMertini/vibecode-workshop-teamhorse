01 / Make something useful

An idea is a start.
A working demo is proof.

Your app is running. Now pick one person, one problem and one action you can help them complete.

  1. 01

    Agree the goal.

    Write who it helps and what they can do. Challenge what you are assuming.

  2. 02

    Build one step.

    Give the agent an outcome and cases to check. Keep the change small enough to review.

  3. 03

    Watch it work.

    Review the change. Let someone try the task. Use what happens to choose your next step.
